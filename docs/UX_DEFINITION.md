# AAAAT desktop interaction

This guide derives from current Product Owner intent and `PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md`. Prior screens and tests do not create product meaning.

Welcome is the first screen. It shows the last loaded workspace, offers another folder, and gives compact honest status for workspace, optional AI and local PDF readiness. Closing Welcome enters Focus over the application corpus. Focus and the complete-data register are two presentations of the same applications, never different application classes or workflows.

A single **New application** action opens one capture screen. Raw pasted or written material and editable useful fields appear side by side on desktop; on narrow screens the fields may fold under **Show fields**. One **Save application** retains any sparse material: just a company, a role, a note, or a pasted offer is sufficient. Optional checkboxes for AI parsing, dedicated CV and cover letter run after local save. Missing AI never prevents save. AI suggestions never replace values the user explicitly entered. A selected CV or cover letter is linked to the saved application and can be opened there later. **CVs** centres reusable CVs. Application letters live with their application; standalone letters are a secondary exception.

Focus supports quick recognition, search and reading of the selected context. **All data** gives a compact complete register of the same corpus and opens deliberate application maintenance directly. Detailed field-definition controls and status history are secondary. **My information** begins with reusable professional facts; grouping and variations are advanced choices, not entry requirements. Common career groups are suggestions, never a closed taxonomy.

The useful information takes the viewport. Titles and repeated explanations are compact. Decoration may give AAAAT character without consuming the space needed for editing and reading. Settings shows incorrect AI addresses and failed validation at the point of use. Bounded external assistance remains host/provider agnostic. Installer and configurator actions act only through typed local capabilities.

Natural-use review should establish that a person can save sparse work, create or find documents, recognize failures, switch workspace and understand the current screen without knowing AAAAT's development vocabulary.
