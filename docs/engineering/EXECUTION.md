# Execution aid

This guide is advisory. It cannot change Product Definition, derived SPEC, Current Mission, Issue scope, or product authority.

Use the GitHub-capable agent for normal bounded implementation, documentation, Issue/PR coordination, CI inspection, and independent review. Use local/Codex work only when shell, Electron, package, browser/visual, TeX/PDF, rendering, filesystem/process, or environment evidence is actually required. The Product Owner is an intentional transport and product reviewer, not routine QA.

Reuse successful verification evidence when intervening changes cannot affect the behavior, platform path, fixture contract, environment assumption, or other premise it proves. Run fresh evidence only for affected surfaces or a concrete gap, and follow the selected lanes in `.github/workflows/verify.yml`.

At a real handoff, begin with:

```text
Now: what works or changed
Next: one bounded outcome
Owner attention: none, or one concrete question
Evidence: Issue/PR and verification conclusion
```

Then provide the repository, authority links, exact Issue/branch, goal, in-scope and excluded work, applicable existing evidence, only still-required checks, and the expected return: URLs, commit SHA, check conclusions, review findings, merge SHA, and blockers.

Keep transient coordination in GitHub. Do not commit task-specific prompts, transcripts, acceptance ledgers, status databases, or generated reports.
