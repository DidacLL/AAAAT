Get started

How to use AAAAT

AAAAT helps you keep track of job opportunities and make CVs and cover letters. This guide walks through the actual desktop app, from opening it to making a document.

Follow along with an opportunity of your own, or choose Open demo to explore the example workspace. You don't need to set up AI before you start.

1. Open AAAAT

  1. Download AAAAT for your computer, install it and open the app.
  2. Choose New workspace. Select an empty folder where you want to keep your applications and documents.
  3. Once the workspace opens, you'll see the main areas in the left navigation: Applications for opportunities, My information for your career details, and Documents for CVs and letters.

Just looking around? Instead of New workspace, choose Open demo on the welcome screen. AAAAT will ask where to create a separate example workspace. Its sample applications, career information and documents are there for you to inspect and try; they aren't real companies you need to apply to.

Applications

2. Keep a job offer

When you find an interesting offer online, you can keep it in AAAAT instead of relying on open browser tabs or trying to remember the details later.

  1. Copy the job posting from the website, or copy a message you've received from a recruiter.
  2. In AAAAT, open Applications and choose New in the top bar.
  3. Paste the text into the Application text area on the left. On the right, add the organization and job title, plus any other details you want to remember, such as location or pay.
  4. Choose Save. The job posting and the details you entered are now together in your workspace.

You can also enter details without pasting an offer, or save just the text and fill in the rest later.

Find it again

3. Recognize an opportunity when you need it

Imagine the recruiter calls a few weeks later. You might not remember every offer you've seen, but AAAAT can help you recover the right one.

  1. Open Applications. You'll see the opportunities you've saved as individual covers.
  2. Use Search to find a company, a role, or a word from the original offer.
  3. Choose the matching cover to see more of its information. Use Open / Edit to read the full details or add a note about the conversation.

Try searching for a company in the demo workspace if you don't have your own saved applications yet.

My information

4. Keep your career information ready

You'll probably use the same experience, skills and education across several applications. Keep those details in My information so you don't have to type them again for every CV.

  1. Open My information from the left navigation.
  2. Add the details you'd normally put on a CV: your name and contact information, jobs you've done, education, skills or projects.
  3. Start with a few useful items. You can return and add more whenever you need them.

If you opened the demo, explore the example person's information to see how it can be reused in documents.

Documents

5. Make a CV

You can make a CV even if you haven't saved a single job application.

  1. Open Documents. At the top, use New CV from to choose My information, or choose Blank if you want to start from scratch.
  2. Choose New CV. Edit the text, include the relevant experience, and arrange the sections until the document says what you want.
  3. Choose Save to keep the editable CV.
  4. Choose PDF when you're ready to produce a document to send. If AAAAT needs a PDF tool first, follow the setup offered in Settings → Documents and then return to your CV.

6. Write a cover letter

To write a letter without opening an application, go to Documents and choose New letter. Write the letter in the editor, save it and generate a PDF when ready.

If the letter belongs to a specific job, open that application and create it there instead. This keeps the documents for that opportunity easy to find together.

That's the everyday workflow

You can now save a job offer, find it again, keep career information and create documents. None of this requires AI.

When you want to go further, AAAAT can help you tailor CVs, create reusable document templates, organize applications with Tags or work with your preferred AI. Those options are explained in Explore AAAAT.
