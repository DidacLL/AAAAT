Get started with AAAAT

From a job offer to something useful.

Follow a complete example, using only ordinary desktop actions. Start by saving an opportunity or go straight to making a CV. You do not need an AI account or technical experience.

Save a practice offerMake your first CV

1. Open your own workspace

  1. Download AAAAT for Windows, macOS or Linux. Install and launch the desktop application. You do not need to install a programming language.
  2. On the welcome screen, choose to create a workspace. When AAAAT asks for a location, select a new empty folder you can find again, for example a folder called My Job Applications in Documents. This is where AAAAT keeps your work.
  3. If AAAAT offers optional AI or PDF tools, choose Not now for this exercise. You can add them later. Once the workspace opens, look for Applications, Documents and My information in the left navigation.

Want to explore without entering anything? The welcome screen also offers fictional demo data in a separate empty folder. It is a sample workspace with example job opportunities and documents, not an account or an import of your information. The exercises below work in a new, empty workspace.

Beginner lesson A · No AI required

Save a job offer. Find it when the recruiter calls.

The situation: you see an interesting role, but you do not have time to apply. Instead of leaving it in browser tabs or scattered notes, you want to keep the original posting and recognize it again in seconds.

What you will finish with: one saved application that you can search, open, and use later to prepare a CV or letter.

Step 1 — Copy this fictional offer

Select and copy the complete text inside this example. It is invented for practice, with no real employer or application to send.

Harborline Logistics
Operations Coordinator
Manchester, UK · Hybrid
Salary: £34,000–£39,000

Help our operations team coordinate deliveries, maintain supplier information, prepare weekly reports and resolve everyday scheduling problems. We are looking for someone who communicates clearly, works comfortably with spreadsheets and can keep several moving tasks organized.

Apply with a CV and a short cover letter.

Step 2 — Create the application

  1. In AAAAT, open Applications from the left navigation. Choose + New in the top bar.
  2. On the New application screen, find the large Application text area on the left and paste the complete practice offer.
  3. On the right, fill in the information that will help you recognize this opportunity: Organisation: Harborline Logistics; Role: Operations Coordinator; Location: Manchester, UK · Hybrid; Compensation: £34,000–£39,000. Leave anything you do not know empty.
  4. Choose Save in the top bar. You have now retained both the posting and the useful details. No AI is required to do any of this.

Step 3 — Find it again

  1. Return to Applications. In the search box, type Harborline (or Operations Coordinator).
  2. Find the saved application's cover. The employer and role should be recognizable without opening the full posting. Select the cover to expand it and inspect more of the information you saved.
  3. Use Open / Edit when you want to add a note, correct a detail, or review the original material. For example, note: Ask the recruiter how often the team works on site.

Result: the next time you hear “Harborline Logistics,” you can search once and recover the role, compensation, original offer and your note.

Beginner lesson B · Start here independently

Make a CV without saving any job offers.

Maybe you just need a new CV. That's a complete reason to open AAAAT. You do not need to finish lesson A.

Step 1 — Start with a blank CV

  1. Open Documents from the left navigation. In the top bar, find the New CV from selector.
  2. Choose Blank, then select New CV. AAAAT opens the editable CV. Starting blank is easiest for this first exercise; later you can begin from My information or a saved template.
  3. Add a short profile about yourself and an example of your experience using the editor's Add information and section editing controls. Use real information when making your own CV. For practice, you could write Operations assistant with experience preparing reports, coordinating schedules and supporting colleagues.
  4. Continue by adding one experience entry and any useful skills or education. Give the document a meaningful title, then choose Save when you want to keep it.

Step 2 — Produce a document you can send

  1. When the CV content looks right, choose PDF in the top bar.
  2. If PDF generation is not ready, open Settings → Documents → PDF setup to use an existing PDF tool or install one with the guided setup. Then return to your saved CV and choose PDF again.
  3. Open the generated PDF and read it as an employer would. Correct the editable CV if something needs improvement and generate an updated file.

Result: you have an editable CV and a finished PDF. Later, keep your experience in My information to reuse it across several documents, or create a cover letter from the Documents area.

Ready to do more?

You can now use AAAAT for two independent everyday tasks: remembering a job opportunity and creating a CV. You can also write cover letters, reuse experience, define Tags and request optional AI assistance.
