Get started

Make AAAAT yours.

Open the app, choose what you want to do, and see how your applications and documents fit together. You can explore with the included demo or work with your own information from the start.

Open AAAAT and get ready

  1. Download AAAAT for your computer, install it, and open the desktop app.
  2. On first launch, AAAAT offers PDF generation and Local AI. Choose what you'll use: PDF generation lets you turn CVs and letters into files you can send; Local AI can help with job-offer details and writing without a paid AI subscription. Select either or both and choose Set up. If PDF tools are already installed, AAAAT can use them. Local AI may require an additional download of about 2 GB. Follow any system installation prompts.
  3. When you're ready, choose Open demo to look around with example information, or New workspace to use your own information. Choose an empty folder for the workspace. Your information will be kept there, on your computer.

Only need to browse job offers for now? You can choose Not now for tools you don't need, and enable them whenever you're ready. Nothing about normal application management requires AI. For PDF documents you will need PDF generation set up.

Explore what AAAAT looks like

Want to see AAAAT in action before entering anything? Choose Open demo on the welcome screen. AAAAT creates a separate workspace filled with fictional examples: job opportunities, professional experience and documents. It's safe to look around and make changes there; your own information isn't involved.

  1. Open Applications in the left navigation. Each cover represents an opportunity. Choose one to see more information.
  2. Try Search with a company name or job title shown on a cover. Open the result and read the saved details and original material.
  3. Open My information to see how career experience and skills are kept for reuse, then open Documents to see the CVs and letters prepared from that information.

You don't have to use the demo to get started. A new, empty workspace works just as well for saving an opportunity or making a CV.

Save an interesting job offer

Found an opportunity online? Keep a copy of the offer so you can return to it whenever you need to. You don't need to apply immediately or fill in every detail.

  1. Copy the text of the job posting, or a message from a recruiter.
  2. In AAAAT, open Applications and choose New from the top bar.
  3. Paste into Application text on the left. On the right, add the organization, job title or other details that would help you recognize it later. If you don't know something, leave it blank.
  4. Choose Save. The original offer and anything you added are kept together.

Prefer to enter the details yourself? That's fine too. You can save just a few words or a useful fragment and add the rest another day.

Find it again when somebody calls

A recruiter mentions a company you spoke to weeks ago. Instead of searching through messages, go back to AAAAT.

  1. Open Applications and use Search to look for the employer, role or another detail you remember.
  2. Choose the matching cover to see more. Select Open / Edit when you want the original offer, saved notes or complete information.
  3. After the call, add a note about what you discussed so it will be there next time.

You can customize which details stand out on an application cover. The advanced guide explains how.

Make a CV and cover letter

You don't need an application saved to make documents. AAAAT can start with reusable career information or a blank CV.

  1. Open My information and add the experience, skills and education you'll want to use in your CV. If you're in the demo workspace, there's already an example profile you can explore.
  2. Open Documents. In New CV from, choose My information to reuse what you've saved, or Blank to start fresh. Select New CV.
  3. Edit the content, change the section order and save the CV when you're happy with it.
  4. Choose PDF in the top bar to generate a finished document. If PDF generation isn't ready yet, open Settings → Documents → PDF setup and complete the setup, then return to the CV.
  5. To write a letter, choose New letter in Documents. You can also prepare a CV and letter from inside a saved application when you want them associated with that opportunity.

Use AI when it helps

AI can help you fill information from a long job posting or work on a CV or letter. If you installed Local AI at the beginning, it's available from within AAAAT. You can also configure another compatible provider or use a text-capable LLM through copy and paste—no dedicated integration needed.

  1. Open Applications → New and paste a job offer.
  2. Choose Fill to ask your configured AI to suggest useful details. Review what it adds and change anything you want.
  3. Save the application. You can always work manually, with or without AI.

For other AI options, see Explore AAAAT.

Keep going at your own pace

AAAAT can be just a place to remember opportunities, just a CV workshop, or both. You can explore reusable Tags, document variations, AI connections and backups whenever you want more control.
