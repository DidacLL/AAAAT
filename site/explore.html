A little more depth

Your information. Your way of working.

These are independent tasks. Read the parts you need; there is no prescribed application workflow.

Keep the source. Add the useful details.

An application is a container for whatever is worth remembering about one opportunity: copied offers, recruiter messages, notes, URLs, fields, Tags and documents. Sparse information is normal.

Sources preserve the original material. Extracting details or asking AI to summarize it does not replace that material.

Common fields are starting points: add or edit field definitions when your search needs something different. Favourites, ordering and prominence make covers recognizable without changing the underlying information.

Try this: save only a recruiter-message fragment. Add the role later, keeping the message intact.

Define a Tag once. Reuse its meaning.

A shared Tag can have a name, aliases, a definition and notes. Attach it across opportunities and read its meaning in the left monitor when you inspect a cover.

The demo’s Human-readable Tag means “A posting that describes the work before describing its passion for describing the work.” A small joke, and a useful reminder of what you value in an offer.

Try this: define a working arrangement once and attach it to two opportunities.

Write it once. Reuse it thoughtfully.

My information holds reusable experience, skills, education, achievements, preferences and anything else relevant to your career. Rename the supplied groups or add your own.

Save variations when an experience deserves different wording or emphasis. Changing a CV does not automatically rewrite your reusable information.

The fictional Alex Morgan profile includes a custom Community work group for repair-café volunteering and a Public-facing emphasis variation. The same background can support community programmes, editorial work or visitor services without inventing a different person.

Compose, edit and keep the output.

Start a CV from reusable information, a saved composition template or a blank page. Write a cover letter independently or inside an application. Arrange sections and choose the wording that fits the document.

Templates preserve reusable composition. Editable CVs and letters remain separate from generated PDFs. In an application, a selected CV and letter can also produce a combined document.

A generated project keeps the document data and exact presentation sources used. Export source project lets advanced users reproduce or adapt the LaTeX project outside AAAAT. Presentation files are called Blueprints; a composition template decides content, while a Blueprint decides presentation.

Ask for help where it is useful.

AI is optional. One selected connection can help extract details from Sources, suggest Tags, work on a field or draft document content. You can continue manual work and edit the results.

Settings → AI configures the connection. Optional Local AI setup installs llama.cpp with the tested Granite model; you can instead configure a compatible service you choose. Providers may have their own costs or credentials.

AAAAT validates returned structure and protects newer manual edits. A fluent answer still needs your judgment. Reachability checks do not certify a model’s usefulness.

Bring the task to another assistant.

Open an application’s ⋯ → AI task. Inspect and edit the complete request, then Copy or Export its visible text to your preferred text-capable LLM. Bring useful material back into your application work.

This manual copy-paste route does not require an API key, MCP support or a special AAAAT integration. The external assistant’s own data-handling choices still apply.

Compatible external tools can also use bounded connected operations, including AAAAT’s local MCP transport. Host-specific setup varies; connected support is not a promise that every assistant has been verified. Future connected reads are an explicit choice. Turning them off cannot recall material already copied or read.

Keep control of the workspace.

Your authoritative information lives in a local folder you choose. Local storage, visual prominence and permission to use information with AI are separate decisions. Remote assistance sends the allowed context to your chosen service.

Settings → Backup creates a consistent workspace backup and restores into an empty destination. Portable AI configuration has a separate export; workspace backups exclude connection credentials.

Reset can clear applications, My information or both while preserving retained generated PDFs and source projects. Keep a backup before intentionally clearing your work.
