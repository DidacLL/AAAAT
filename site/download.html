First launch

Make AAAAT ready for what you want to do.

When you open AAAAT for the first time, it offers the tools for making PDFs and getting local AI help. Choose the capabilities you want—both can be set up together.

Make finished CVs and letters.

Choose PDF generation if you want to produce documents ready to send. AAAAT can use a compatible PDF tool already on your computer or guide you through installing one. Your CV remains editable in AAAAT.

Get AI help on your computer.

Choose Local AI if you want help working with job postings and document text without paying an AI provider. The optional model download is about 2 GB. AI isn't required for normal editing or application management.

Explore or begin your own work.

Choose Open demo to try AAAAT with fictional applications and CVs, or New workspace to work with your own information. You'll choose a local folder for either one.

Choose Set up for the selected tools, and follow any installation prompts from your operating system. If you only need the manual workspace today, you can return to these options through Settings. The getting-started guide walks through the whole first visit.

A small project with open doors

Need help getting started?

Use the illustrated guide to find your way around AAAAT. If something does not work as expected, you can report it to the open-source project. Please keep personal application information out of public reports.
