Set up what you need, when you need it

A workspace first.
Optional tools later.

Basic application work and document editing do not require an AI connection. PDF generation needs a compatible pdfLaTeX installation.

01 / YOUR FOLDER

Create or open a workspace.

Pick a folder under your control. To explore with fictional data, choose the demo option and an empty folder of its own.

Follow the short guide →
02 / YOUR DOCUMENTS

Add PDF generation.

Use optional TeX setup or point AAAAT at an existing compatible pdfLaTeX executable. The app delegates installation to native tools.

CV and letter content stays editable; generated PDFs and portable document projects are yours.

Document work explained →
03 / YOUR CHOICE

Add AI if it helps.

Configure your chosen connection, use optional local-AI setup, or copy an editable task into a text-capable LLM you already use.

Existing providers remain your choice. An AI account or API key is not required to use AAAAT itself.
