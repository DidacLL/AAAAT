# AAAAT

AAAAT is a local-first desktop workspace for managing job-search operations and the material around them.

Its public product direction is practical and privacy-conscious: keep application work, reusable professional context, preparation, and generated artifacts under local ownership instead of turning the workflow into a hosted tracker.

## Public scope

The repository documents the application’s product principles, local-workspace approach, and implementation. It does not make private candidature records or generated application material part of the public site contract.
